Weft: Habit Tracker

A small, open-source habit tracker for Android. Check off your habits, log part of a day, and keep a short note. Your data stays on your phone.

Get it on Google Play Download the APK

What Weft does

  • Each day, every habit is Done, Partial or Skipped. Swipe right for Done, left for Skip, or press and hold to log an amount, like 15 of 20 pages.
  • See your week at a glance, or the last 26 weeks as a heatmap, with streaks.
  • Keep a Journal: a short note for any habit and day.
  • Insights for any dates: scores by weekday and by habit.
  • Reminders, four home-screen widgets, light and dark themes, five fonts, seven languages.
  • Export to a JSON or CSV file, and optional backup to your own Google Drive.

Your data

Weft has no account, no ads, no analytics and no server. Your habits, check-ins and notes are stored only on your phone.

Google Drive backup is optional and off until you tap Connect Google Drive (Google Play and GitHub versions). Weft then asks Google for two things:

  • Its own hidden folder in your Google Drive (drive.appdata), to keep your last 10 backups there. Weft can't see any other file in your Drive.
  • Your email address, to show which account the backups go to.

Backups go straight from your phone to your own Drive. We never receive them. Disconnect or delete the backups at any time in Weft's Backup & restore screen. Details are in the privacy policy.
